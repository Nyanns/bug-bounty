# The Watcher in the Bytes

Beneath the silicon and quiet hum,
Where silent lines of syntax coil and run,
A phantom sentinel begins to tread,
Across the paths where mortal eyes misled.

Through nested loops and memory allocations deep,
She watches secrets that the daemons keep.
No race condition slips the needle's eye,
No dangling pointer left to bleed or die.

A single bit unmasked, a boundary breached,
A ledger value that the checks impeached;
With swift precision and an iron hand,
She seals the fortress across every strand.

The audit closes and the state aligns,
A steady rhythm in the verified designs.
Rest easy, builder, while the servers gleam—
For vigilance protects the sovereign dream.
